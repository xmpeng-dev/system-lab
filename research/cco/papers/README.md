# Publication planning

These are prospective public research themes, not announcements or claims of novelty.

1. Tile-level communication–computation fusion for context and expert parallelism: dependency patterns, specialized schedules, forward/backward and controlled ablations.
2. Heterogeneous communication–computation overlap on scale-up GPU systems: transport selection, granularity, resource contention and forward progress.
3. Adaptive communication–computation scheduling: workload-dependent fusion and transport selection.

For each: literature audit, research question, claim/evidence ledger, fair baselines, correctness, negative results, limitations and reproducibility. Manuscripts, unpublished titles, data and submission plans require disclosure approval before publication.
